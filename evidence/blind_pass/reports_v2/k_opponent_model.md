## Findings, by severity

### 1. `positional_forfeits` sums a *conditional hazard* as an *expected count* — `expected_taken` exceeds the number of intervening picks and triples the convention it reads from
**draft_strategy.py:471-502** (`pace_p = position_pace_probability(position, picks_made_now + offset, picks, ...)`; `if pace_p > p_position: p_position = pace_p`; `expected_taken += p_position`).

`position_pace_probability` advances the *expected* cumulative count with `offset` while `actual_now` is read off the fixed `picks` list, so the deficit grows with every hypothetical intervening pick. That is a hazard conditional on nothing having been taken yet — defensible inside `estimate_survival`'s per-player product (its docstring argues exactly that), but `positional_forfeits` sums it across the gap and calls the sum the expected number of players taken. Measured (n = intervening picks, all gaps AHEAD, checked `my_next − current − 1` == reported at every state):

| state | gap | QB `expected_taken` | convention's own bound (`expected_position_pace(now+gap) − actual`) | real QBs taken in that gap | all-position total / picks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.01, 0 picks | 22 | **15.54** | 8.92 | 5 | **26.15 / 22** |
| 2.01, 12 picks | 11 | 4.51 | 4.21 | 1 | 10.38 / 11 |
| 2.12, 23 picks | 14 | **10.28** | 5.67 (increment 2.46) | 6 | **16.76 / 14** |
| 4.01, 35 picks | 25 | 3.54 | 2.50 | — | 14.93 / 25 |

Past the last anchor (48 picks) pace returns None and totals fall to 8.9-11.4 per 18-22 picks, i.e. the defect is confined to rounds 1-4 in superflex, which is where the QB decision lives. The `#206` docstring on this very function lists "expected_taken exceeds the picks available" as the arithmetic impossibility it repaired; the pace max-in re-broke it. Consequences at 1.01: QB `positional_forfeit` 58.5 (curve walked 15.5 QBs deep instead of ≤ 9), `position_next_turn_value` 104.4, `acting_now_value` 58.5 for Josh Allen, `forfeit_component` in necessity, and the debate/UI sentence "Waiting on him costs about 58.5 universal-value points by your next turn". At 2.12 the QB forfeit is 40.4 with the curve walked to the 11th QB where the convention says the 6th. Survival is withheld from humans, but forfeit, next-turn value and acting-now are not, and necessity labels feed `draft_counterfactual.classify_deviation` ("MUST TAKE"/"STRONG ACTION" ⇒ deviation supported). Fix shape: bound the summed pace mass by the convention's increment over the gap (or compute E[N] from the hazards properly), not a per-position cap.

### 2. `block_opportunity`'s credible-path gate is unreachable for every non-pace take since the #206 normalisation — the "Denies a rival" force is dead
**pick_synthesis.py:480, 1060, 1070** (`CREDIBLE_RIVAL_PATH_THRESHOLD = 0.10`; `credible_rival_path = take_prob >= 0.10`). The constant's comment says it means "roughly rank-4-or-better under RANK_TAKE_PROBABILITY" — true of the raw table (rank 4 = 0.10), false after `board_take_mass` normalises over ~960 rows: rank-1 becomes 0.55/20.5-22.6 = **0.024-0.027**. Measured across 9 states: 4,812 rank-based take probabilities, max **0.032**, n ≥ 0.10 = **0**; the only probabilities ≥ 0.10 are pace-driven superflex QBs (320 of 855). `rival_premium ≥ 8` fired on **184 of 369** candidates (every RB and WR at early states — the premium is a position-level constant on empty rosters), `block_opportunity` was True on **0 of 369**. The flag reaches the UI as force `"block"` → "Denies {team}, who has nothing comparable behind him" (draft_board_ui.py:81, 777-830). Scenario: 1QB league, a rival with an empty RB1 slot has your RB at rank 1 on their board with premium 9.0 — the label cannot fire because 0.55/total_weight ≈ 0.025. The gate's own justification (the ablation experiment) predates the change of units.

### 3. Pre-turn preview computes every gap-dependent quantity over the interval *after* the user's turn, while the strings say "before your next selection"
**app.py:5370, 5436** (`target_index = find_next_pick_index(pick_order, my_roster_id, current_index − 1)`; `build_snapshot(current_index=target_index, picks=draft_picks)`). When it is not the user's turn, the snapshot is built as if it were, with the board still containing players who will go before the user picks, and `intervening_picks`/forfeit/necessity computed from target → next. Arithmetic (plain snake, 12 teams, seat 12 at 1.01, 0 picks made): target 11, next 12, intervening **0**, so every candidate gets `survival_basis = no_intervening_picks` ("you pick again immediately — nobody picks in between") and `positional_forfeits` returns `{}` — while 11 picks are about to happen. Measured on the real order (Snoopking51 owning 1.12, 0 picks made): board led by Gibbs/Robinson/McCaffrey, "6 pick(s) to your next selection" (draft_board_ui.py:61), RB forfeit 7.3 — the gap that decides whether any of those three exists at 1.12 is 11 picks and is nowhere in the numbers. The "Not your turn yet" caption (app.py:5472) does not relabel the quantities. The cache key does include `current_index` and `picks` (null result on the cache).

### 4. The positional-run detector fires on ~29% of real states and was anti-predictive for QB and RB on the one real draft
**draft_strategy.py:146** (`RUN_LOOKBACK 4 / RUN_THRESHOLD 3`) feeding a 1.6× weight (line 74) and `NECESSITY_RUN_BONUS = 6.0` (pick_synthesis.py:331, 816). Applying the production detector to GSOP2's 307 resolved picks in order: fired at **89/307 states (29.0%)**; the next pick matched the run position 29/89. Per position: QB 1/12 (base rate 0.18), RB 5/26 (0.28), WR 22/46 (0.37), TE 1/5 (0.17). One league, not a benchmark, but it is the only observed evidence and it points the other way for two of four positions (a run that just cleared a tier lowers, not raises, the next-pick hazard). Under normalisation the boost moves rank-1 from 0.027 to ~0.034, so its survival effect is small; the +6 necessity points are the live effect.

### 5. `draft_counterfactual._full_board` prices vendor-only; a sleeper-priced trajectory would be scored against a different price system (latent)
**draft_counterfactual.py:117, 179** — `compute_draft_board(...)` with no `sleeper_projections`/`sleeper_basis`/`weekly_projections`/`upside_rule`, and `trajectory.config["priced_from"]` is never read. At 1.01 on GSOP2: vendor-only board 259 priced rows vs production 481; **50/50** of production's top priced rows carry a different `final_score`; Gibbs is BPA on both but `final_score` 214.92 vs 241.86, so `regret_vs_bpa` for taking the BPA himself would read **+26.94**. Latent today: every caller (`run_counterfactual_analysis.py`, `run_out_of_sample_validation.py`, `run_95d2111_effect_report.py`, …) feeds vendor-only trajectories built on the 764-row synthetic-id vendor pool, and `draft_battery.py` never calls `compare_trajectory`. It becomes live the moment a battery trajectory is handed to it — the same #214/F2 class already fixed in `pick_analysis` and `simulate_opponent_picks`.

### 6. The survival model inside simulated drafts describes rivals that the simulation does not contain
`draft_simulation.simulate_full_draft` rivals take `candidates[0]` with certainty; `estimate_survival` prices each rival's take of their own rank-1 at 0.024-0.027. At 1.01, Gibbs is rank 1 on **11/11** intervening boards (all eleven boards are identical at the empty-roster state, so "each team's own board" is one board), model survival **0.55**; harness survival is 0 at 1.02. Since survival is withheld and necessity has no selection authority, picks are unaffected; what is affected is what the trajectory *records* (necessity labels, opportunity cost, acting-now) and, through `classify_deviation`, the counterfactual's "deviation supported" verdicts. Coherence finding, not a wrong pick.

### 7. Calibration of the take model against observed behaviour (repo's own data, restated with n)
`observed_take_distribution.json`: 276 measured human picks; rank-1 share 0.022, top-5 0.149, within-top-5 counts 6/8/8/8/11 — flat-to-rising, not the 0.55→0.06 shape of `RANK_TAKE_PROBABILITY`; ~50% of picks landed at engine rank ≤ 24, where the normalised model puts ~7% of its mass (floor spread over ~960 rows). `calibration_real_rank.json`: 6,616 pairs, Brier 0.161 vs constant-base-rate 0.145, `beats_constant: false`; the 0.0-0.1 predicted bucket (n=73) observed 0.48 — consistent with Finding 1's pace hazard (those near-zero predictions are pace-driven QBs; at 2.12 the model gave the 4 best remaining QBs survival 0.000/0.002/0.019/0.058 across 14 picks in which 6 QBs actually went). The repo already withholds survival on this basis; recorded here because the same hazard also drives the un-withheld forfeit.

## Null results (examined carefully, nothing found)
- Interval selection: `estimate_survival` and `positional_forfeits` both use `pick_order[current+1 : my_next]` — the gap AHEAD; reported `intervening_picks` equalled `my_next − current − 1` at all 9 states (6, 11, 14, 18, 22, 22, 22, 25). `find_next_pick_index` / `intervening_roster_ids` were correct on the real traded order (82 trades). `generate_pick_order("3rr")` reproduces the board's observed round shapes (asc/desc/desc/asc).
- Opponent boards are priced with the caller's `sleeper_projections`/basis/`weekly_projections` (481 priced on my board and on every rival board); `rival_premium = final_score − universal_value` is computed on the rival's own board with the rival's roster, as the consuming necessity term assumes.
- Mock-draft rivals (`simulate_opponent_picks` → `board[0]`) and simulation rivals (`narrow_candidates[0]` via `_board_order`) sort on the same key tuple (feasible, unfieldable, score, unpriced-last, player_id).
- Denial/rival-premium three-state absence handling (`no_intervening_rival` / `no_rival_priced` / `measured`) is consistent between producer and consumers; `_board_take_mass_cached` is per-board, dies with the analysis.
