### 1. HIGH — The "cannot be fielded" backstop has no bound for any position reachable through a shared slot, although the bound is derivable from the same config
`draft_room.fieldable_ceiling` (draft_room.py:3442-3470), `unfieldable_last` (3473-3611), and the battery audit `draft_battery.unfieldable_depth` (394-447) ceiling a position only when *every* slot admitting it admits it alone. The docstring's reason ("a spare RB fills a FLEX and frees a WR upward") is about usefulness *below* the bound, not the bound: at most `|slots admitting P|` players at P can start in any week regardless of what else is rostered, so `slots_reachable(P) + 1` is exactly as derivable as the dedicated case and invents no constant. Today a flex-reachable position gets *no* ceiling at all.

**Measured failure (real capture, 12-team, `["QB","RB","RB","WR","WR","TE","FLEX","IDP_FLEX"] + 6 BN`, 14 rounds, `simulate_opponent_picks` drafting all 12 seats):** `fieldable_ceiling` returns `{'QB': 2}` only. Final rosters: team 2 holds 6 IDP, team 3 holds 6, team 4 holds 7, team 5 holds 7, team 11 holds 7, team 12 holds 7 — against ONE startable IDP slot (ceiling would be 2). Every roster reports 8/8 starting slots filled, so `feasibility_first`, `unfilled_starting_slots` and `unfieldable_last` are all silent. IDP picks by round: 6:3, 7:4, 8:5, 9:6, 10:6, 11:5, 12:6, 13:7, 14:6. The owner's own league (2× IDP_FLEX, SUPER_FLEX) gets `fieldable_ceiling == {'K': 2}` — no IDP bound and no QB bound — and draft_battery.py:178-179 already records "102 IDP players into 24 IDP slots … `structural_findings` = 0 throughout, because every one of those rosters is LEGAL" (8.5 IDP/team vs a derivable 3). This is the nine-defense pathology the backstop was built for, reappearing at every position the backstop exempts.

### 2. HIGH — Roster fill is counted by primary label, so `need_bonus` and league-wide starter demand mis-read every multi-eligible player
`team_filled_by_position` (draft_room.py:963-981) and `_team_starters_filled` (2332-2341) count each pick under `player_position(info)` (= first listed fantasy position). `score_row` (≈4205-4225) then subtracts that from `dedicated_slot_counts[position]` where `position` is the *pool bucket* (`_pool_position`, 1333-1366), which can differ from the primary. `remaining_starter_demand` (983-1031) feeds the same census into the replacement anchor. Population on this capture: 164 IDP duals (127 DL/LB, 37 DB/LB), 3 DB/WR, 3 RB/TE, etc.

**Measured (HEAVY_IDP `DL,DL,LB,LB,DB,DB`, roster = Mazi Smith DL, Calijah Kancey DL, Nolan Smith DL/LB, Myles Murphy DL/LB):** `my_filled = {'DL': 4}`; `need_bonus` is **8.0 on all 286 LB rows** and 0.0 on all 213 DL rows, while `optimize_lineup` with full eligibility fills `DL_7, DL_8, LB_9, LB_10` — both LB slots are already covered. The board pushes a 5th front-seven body at +8 (NEED_BONUS_MAX is 12) over the two genuinely open DB slots (also 8.0). **Travis Hunter (12530, `["DB","WR"]`) in an offence-only league:** priced and bucketed as WR (`position: 'WR'`), but once drafted `my_filled = {'DB': 1}` and WR `need_bonus` stays 8.67, identical to an empty roster.

### 3. HIGH — `league_config`'s blocking state is never consulted by any production path
`ambiguities` / `confirmation_state` / `admits_decision` / `decision_config` (league_config.py:164-259) have **zero production callers**; app.py imports the module only for `describe_config_age` (app.py:65, 1707). The docstring promises AMBIGUOUS is "The only blocking state" and that an unknown slot "makes the config AMBIGUOUS" because "the lineup solver cannot place anyone in them" — but `slots_from_roster_positions` (lineup_optimizer.py:52-67) silently drops any unrecognised label and everything downstream builds on the smaller lineup.

**Measured:** league with `[...,"FLEX","DE","CB"] + BN`: `ambiguities` → `unknown_slot`; `compute_draft_board` nonetheless returned 970 rows / 481 priced with a 7-slot lineup (no DE/CB demand, no IDP in pool). Two-literal-QB league (`QB,QB,RB,RB,WR,WR,TE,FLEX`): `ambiguities` → `superflex_disagreement`; `league_format_hint`/`league_format_summary` call it superflex (so the SF rankings *file* is selected), yet the board prices QBs under the 1QB regime — Josh Allen 91.03 `live_starter_demand` vs 170.02 `startable_floor` in the equivalent SUPER_FLEX league (draft_room.py:3801 keys the floor off the token alone; pick_synthesis.py:1711 and draft_strategy.py:179 likewise). The detector that exists for exactly this never runs.

### 4. MEDIUM — `roster_diagnostics` solves lineups with a single label, contradicting the battery's own legality audit on the same roster
roster_diagnostics.py:237 builds `"eligible": {p["position"]}` from the serialized candidate's one `pos`, while `draft_battery.unfilled_starting_slots` (312-316) uses the full `fantasy_positions`. **Measured:** the four-player IDP roster above fills 4 slots under sets and **2 under labels**. So `starting_lineup_value`, `bench_surplus_value`, `bye_collision` and `bye_concentration` (the battery's roster-strength instruments) understate any roster relying on a dual to fill a slot — and the battery can report "all slots filled" beside a diagnostic whose lineup has holes. Same file, line 253: `structural_holes = usable_positions - rostered buckets` reports DL and DB as holes for a roster covering its only `IDP_FLEX` with a LB (owner's league: any roster without a DL is a "structural hole").

Practical exposure on this capture is small (none of the four has a team, so none reaches a board), but it is a one-vocabulary-two-homes defect in exactly the predicate the backstops rely on.
