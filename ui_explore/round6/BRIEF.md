## THE VISUAL DIRECTION — attend to it, do not restart it

Measured off `deck`, which the owner chose. These are the contracts, not suggestions:

- **Type scale spans ~12px to ~44px with real jumps.** The base's first pass put eleven sizes
  inside a 1.97:1 range and read as "everything is the same size".
- **The FIGURE is the hero**, in mono, large, with negative tracking. A glance reads a number,
  not a clause.
- **Cinzel (`design_system.FONT_DISPLAY`) carries the wordmark, eyebrows, labels and buttons** --
  uppercase, heavily letterspaced. **Never numerals**: Cinzel's letterspaced digits turn "11"
  into Roman "II". Every figure is mono.
- **Surfaces are vertical gradients, not flat fills.** A 45-degree hatch at ~6-14% opacity keeps
  a near-black panel from reading as an empty div.
- **Fading gold hairlines** (`linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(117,80,36,.22), rgba(117,80,36,.04))`).
- **Position colours on every player and position**, from `design_system.POSITION_PILL_TOKENS`.
- **The provenance line in mono under each figure** -- `measured - depth_label on value`. Honesty
  made visible, and it doubles as texture.
- Gold is a **gradient** measured off the real logo (`#755024 -> #ba9561 -> #f9de99 -> #fff4ce`,
  body only 39% saturation), never a flat `#c9961a` beside it.

**Use `design_system.root_css_block()` and `position_pill_css_block()` for the palette.** Do not
hardcode hexes that duplicate tokens -- `#312` found one palette with three homes.

## THE HARD LAW — you may not invent a quantity

This project has withdrawn published findings rather than keep a number it could not defend.
**A design that invents a quantity is not a design, it is a liability.**

### Real — you may draw these

| quantity | where it comes from |
|---|---|
| bye damage per week | `lineup_optimizer.bye_collision` -- value lost, not bodies |
| share of the season's bye damage in the worst week | `bye_concentration` |
| thin positions | `depth_ratings` WEAK + NO_PLAYERS, peer-relative |
| positional depth **label** | `depth_label` -> exactly `Strong`/`Average`/`Weak`/`None - no rostered players here` |
| counts rostered, and how many could be PRICED | `positional_depth` cell: `count`, `valued_count` |
| structural holes | `slots_from_roster_positions` + `slot_coverage` |
| who is on bye in week N | a FILTER on `roster[].bye` -- arranging, not computing |
| what the band CHECKED | `front_claims.checks_run` / `checks_summary` |

### Forbidden — in any styling

Team rating, team index, letter grades. Playoff odds, title odds, win probability. Positional
strength as a **0-100 bar or meter** (the LABEL is real; the continuous score is not -- four
discrete steps only). "% rostered", trending adds. Anything about **Questionable** players
(`compute_readiness` excludes it deliberately). A **positive all-clear for injuries** (absence of
a flag is not evidence of health). Rookie pick **value**. **Any rank of teams within a position**
-- `depth_label` is a ratio band against the league MEAN and nothing ranks teams; asserting a
rank is the defect `#336b` records.

### Absence has a grammar

A value not measured renders as an **em dash carrying its reason**, never `0`, never blank, never
a reassuring default. A floor says so (`>=`). A fabricated-for-demo value is stamped
`CONSTRUCTED`. Four states, visually distinct.

**And an absence must read as INFORMATION, not as damage.** A bare dash where a name should be
reads as a broken template. An absence identical on every row is noise -- state it once.

## Rulings already made by the owner — honour them

1. **Expansions are POPOVERS, not inline disclosure.** "a popout or pop-over, not in-line."
2. **Record is OFF the front page.** `team_standings` returns it cheaply and it is REAL -- and it
   asks nothing of the reader and will say the same thing tomorrow. The test is not "is it real?"
   but **"does it ask anything of me?"**
3. **Five rooms and one dock.** `Matchup` `Roster Maintenance` `Draft Room` `League`
   `Import Audit`, plus the Prytaneum, which is a persistent DOCK and not a sixth room.
4. **The bye figure carries a CURRENCY STAMP.** It is dynasty trade value, not the league's own
   points. The owner has ruled grading belongs in league points; that conversion is unbuilt, so
   the page says what the figure actually is.
5. **The quiet state is the PRIMARY design.** Most weeks the band is empty and that is the
   feature. A quiet page must not read as unfinished -- the first pass did, at three small lines.

## Gates you must pass, and must MEASURE rather than assume

- **Non-text contrast >= 3:1** for every bar, rail and mark, against its own background.
  `design_system.contrast_ratio(token, "bg")` is the instrument. All three round-4 designs failed
  this: five of six bars at 1.25:1-2.30:1, because a LOW value drawn as a DARK gold cannot clear
  the floor on near-black. `line` is 1.27:1 and `line-2` is 1.73:1.
- **No text below 12px computed.** Declare small sizes in `rem`, never `em`, so the floor is
  checkable without a browser.
- **Tap targets >= 44px.**
- **No horizontal overflow at 390px.**
- **Body text >= 4.5:1** on its own background.

Put the first two in your builder as **assertions that fail the build**. The base does; copy the
pattern.
